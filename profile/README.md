## 🇰🇷 소개

AI 덕분에 혼자서도 만들 수 있는 것이 훨씬 많아졌습니다.
그래서 우리는 **각자 프로젝트를 만들고 공유하는 것**을 기본으로 하고,
좋은 아이디어에 사람들이 모이면 **함께 큰 프로젝트**로 키워 갑니다.

### 이런 걸 해요

- 🛠 **개인 프로젝트 공유** — 만들고 있는 것, 만든 것을 자유롭게 보여 주세요. 관심 있는 프로젝트에는 만든 사람의 허락을 받고 합류할 수 있어요.
- 💡 **AI 소식과 노하우** — 새로운 모델, 도구, 작업 방식을 서로 알려 줍니다.
- 🚀 **큰 프로젝트** — [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/sshs-ai-club/discussions/categories/ideas)에 아이디어를 올리고 👍를 모아요. 사람이 모이면 저장소를 만들고 Projects 보드로 함께 관리합니다.

## 🇺🇸 About

With AI, one person can now build far more than before. So our default is
**everyone builds their own projects and shares them** — and when a great idea
attracts enough people, we team up on a **bigger project** together.

### What we do

- 🛠 **Share personal projects** — show what you're building or have built. If something catches your eye, ask the author and join in.
- 💡 **Share AI know-how** — new models, tools and workflows.
- 🚀 **Big projects** — post an idea in [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/sshs-ai-club/discussions/categories/ideas) and collect 👍. Once people gather, we create a repo and run it on a Projects board.

### Current projects

| Project | Description |
|---|---|
| [**manhattan-headtracked-viewer**](https://github.com/sshs-ai-club/manhattan-headtracked-viewer) | A Windows Unity viewer of Manhattan where webcam head tracking turns your monitor into a window onto the city |
| [**origami-compiler**](https://github.com/sshs-ai-club/origami-compiler) | Describe what you want to fold; get foldable 3D designs, a step-by-step instruction book, and a folding video |
| [**spiderman**](https://github.com/sshs-ai-club/spiderman) | A Unity 3D prototype with web-swinging and wall-climbing |

### Join us

1. Say hi in the Issues or Discussions of a project you like.
2. Have your own idea? Post it in [Ideas](https://github.com/orgs/sshs-ai-club/discussions/categories/ideas).
3. Want to become a member? Just ask one of the club officers.
